VPN checks

These checks are separate. A changed IP alone does not prove that DNS or WebRTC cannot leak. Results stay on this page until it closes.

1. Public IP and approximate location

The check contacts ipapi.co through this tab's browser session. The service receives your public IP and returns an approximate location. Take a baseline with the VPN off, then connect and check again. The baseline reveals your normal IP to that service.

2. DNS leak check

A public IP check cannot identify your DNS resolver. This opens a separate third-party DNS test; that site and its DNS servers receive your IP and test queries. Compare the resolvers shown with what you expect for your VPN. This browser does not independently verify the site's results.

3. WebRTC candidates

The test asks WebRTC for candidates and contacts Google's public STUN server. Google may receive your IP, including a direct IP if WebRTC bypasses the proxy. Local candidates can be hidden with mDNS. No candidates or a matching IP does not prove there is no leak.
