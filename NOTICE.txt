PsychoVault development preview

I write the PsychoVault browser, shell and engine code for this project. I use operating-system APIs and my own tests. I do not bundle Chromium, Electron, Gecko, WebView or another third-party browser engine in this preview.

Proton VPN is not bundled or verified in this preview. When I add extensions, the extension code is the explicit third-party exception and will keep its own notices.
