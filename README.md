# PsychoVault

I am rebuilding PsychoVault as my own browser. I want the same interface and features I had before, with my own engine and extension system, and Proton VPN working inside it.

This repository is currently at the planning stage. I have not published a new-engine browser yet.

## Where I am keeping the work

- [PSYCHO.md](PSYCHO.md) - my project guide and non-negotiable requirements.
- [docs/RESEARCH.md](docs/RESEARCH.md) - the research I did before planning the build.
- [docs/PLAN.md](docs/PLAN.md) - my architecture, gates and delivery sequence.
- [docs/FEATURE-INVENTORY.md](docs/FEATURE-INVENTORY.md) - the old features and settings I am keeping myself accountable to.
- [docs/PLAN-AUDIT.md](docs/PLAN-AUDIT.md) - the audit of the plan before implementation.

## What I am not claiming

I am not claiming that a merged plan is a working browser. I am not claiming Proton, privacy, performance or Android support until I have test evidence. I am not using my old screenshots as proof that the new engine is finished.

## Licence and notices

I will keep my own code, bundled extensions, third-party notices and no-warranty information separate and accurate. Proton VPN remains Proton's extension, not my browser engine.
