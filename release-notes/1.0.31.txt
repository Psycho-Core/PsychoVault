Proton VPN background worker

- Fix the extension service worker identity check. Electron supplies the worker as
  event.serviceWorker, not event.sender, so background proxy requests were rejected.
- The earlier 1.0.30 test also fixed the Proton account sign-in tab and made update
  installers go directly to Downloads without a Save As prompt.
- This does not prove that a real Proton server connects or protects traffic. Check your
  public IP before relying on the VPN.
