Local vault safety and sidebar controls

- Hide the local vault's password fields while typing.
- Keep an unreadable or damaged vault locked instead of treating it as empty.
- Require the existing vault to be unlocked before changing or removing its master password.
- The sidebar has a control for PsychoVault's own ad blocker above the Proton VPN button.
- Proton VPN can report Protected while pages do not load. It is not verified as working.
- uBlock Origin Lite is not bundled or verified. The ad-blocker button controls PsychoVault's blocker.
- The local vault changes have not had an independent security audit.
